## 🚀 How to Use This Suite for Future Client Projects

When taking on a new commercial web development client (e.g., a restaurant, logistics company, medical clinic, or real estate agency):

### Step 1: Duplicate Document 15
Copy [`15-New-Client-Project-Template.md`](./15-New-Client-Project-Template.md) into your new client repository as `PROJECT-SPEC.md`. Search and replace all `[PLACEHOLDER]` tags during the client discovery interview.

### Step 2: Follow Document 14 Phase by Phase
Use [`14-Reusable-Website-Development-SOP.md`](./14-Reusable-Website-Development-SOP.md) as your team's operating manual. It gives exact commands, required files, pitfalls to avoid, and step-by-step implementation recipes from Stage 1 (Discovery) to Stage 12 (Client Custody Transfer).

### Step 3: Enforce Quality with Document 16
Check off items in [`16-Complete-Website-Development-Checklist.md`](./16-Complete-Website-Development-Checklist.md) prior to staging deployment, client walkthrough, and production DNS propagation.

### Step 4: Hand Over Smoothly with Document 12
Use [`12-Client-Handover.md`](./12-Client-Handover.md) to package the credentials securely, conduct the training session, and obtain the formal signature on the Project Acceptance Certificate.
